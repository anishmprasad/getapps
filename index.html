The platform layer

Not a bundle of apps.
A system that shares a spine.

Most suites are separate products with a shared logo. GetApps puts identity, billing, permissions and data underneath every app — so adding your ninth tool costs you nothing in setup and nothing in migration.

Single sign-on, one directory, one set of security policies. Invite a teammate once and they land in every app you've enabled — with the right role already applied.

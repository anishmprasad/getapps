Median setup for a first app is twelve minutes. A full multi-tool migration — importing customers, historical invoices and support history from other vendors — is typically five to ten working days, and our migration team does the mapping with you at no cost on Growth and Scale.

Yes, at any time and without asking us. Every record type has a documented JSON and CSV export available from the API and the dashboard, including attachments and audit history. There is no export fee and no notice period.

You choose a primary region when you create the workspace — EU, US, India and Australia on Growth, any of 31 regions on Scale. Data at rest stays in that region; backups are replicated within the same jurisdiction. Region changes are supported once per year with a scheduled cutover.
